Parsha Readings

Parasha Readings – Tzav to V’Zot HaBerachah
בָּלָק
Balak
“Balak”
Shabbat: 06/27/2026

Torah: Numbers 22:2–25:9

Haftarah: Micah 5:6–6:8

Apostolic: Revelation 2:12–17

  1. How does God turn Balak’s curses into blessings?
  2. What subtle strategy is used to lead Israel into sin?
  3. How does Micah summarize what God truly requires?
פִּינְחָס
Pinchas
“Phinehas”
Shabbat: 07/04/2026

Torah: Numbers 25:10–30:1

Haftarah: 1 Kings 18:46–19:21

Apostolic: Romans 11:25–36

  1. Why is Pinchas given a covenant of peace after a violent act?
  2. How do the census and inheritance laws prepare Israel for the Land?
  3. How does Elijah’s story speak to zeal, discouragement, and God’s gentle voice?
מַטּוֹת – מַסְעֵי
Matot – Masei
“Tribes” – “Journeys”
Shabbat: 07/04/2026

Torah: Numbers 30:2–36:13

Haftarah: Jeremiah 2:4–28; 3:4

Apostolic: Hebrews 11:8–16

  1. What do Israel’s recorded journeys reveal about God’s faithfulness?
  2. How do the cities of refuge picture justice and mercy together?
  3. How does Hebrews describe the pilgrim mindset of God’s people?
דְּבָרִים
Devarim
“Words”
Shabbat: 07/11/2026

Torah: Deuteronomy 1:1–3:22

Haftarah: Isaiah 1:1–27

Apostolic: Matthew 4:1–11

  1. Why does Moses retell Israel’s history before entering the Land?
  2. What failures and lessons does he highlight for the new generation?
  3. How does Yeshua’s use of Deuteronomy in the wilderness model obedience?
