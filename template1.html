Hebrew Word Explorer

דֶּרֶךְ
derekh
Way, path, journey.
Root: דרך – to tread, to walk.
Usage: Describes the path one walks, both physically and spiritually.
Reference: Psalm 1:6, Jeremiah 6:16.
תּוֹרָה
Torah
Instruction, teaching, law.
Root: ירה – to shoot, to point out, to instruct.
Usage: The revealed instruction of Elohim for His people.
Reference: Psalm 19:7, Proverbs 6:23.
שָׁלוֹם
shalom
Peace, wholeness, completeness.
Root: שלם – to be complete, sound.
Usage: More than absence of conflict; the fullness of well-being.
Reference: Numbers 6:26, Isaiah 26:3.
