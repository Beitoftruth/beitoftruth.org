Messianic Parshah Readings

Torah • Haftarah • Brit Chadashah

If you see any dates for High Holies that is from the rabbinic calendar and SHOULD ONLY be used as a rough estimate.
We use the Moon to determine when a New Month begins and that tells Karaites when Holy Days will occur as per Leviticus 23.

Feb 28 – Parashat #20 Tetzaveh (Exodus 27:20–30:10)

Theme: Priestly garments and consecration

Messianic Connection: Yeshua as our High Priest clothed in righteousness (Hebrews 4:14–16)

Haftarah: Ezekiel 43:10–27 | Brit Chadashah: Revelation 1:12–20
Psalm 20

Mar 7 – Parashat #21 Ki Tisa (Exodus 30:11–34:35)

Theme: Intercession and renewal

Messianic Connection: Messiah intercedes and restores covenant (Hebrews 9:11–15)

Haftarah: 1 Kings 18:1–39 | Brit Chadashah: 2 Corinthians 3:1–18
Psalm 21

Mar 14 – Parashat #22 Vayakhel (Exodus 35:1–38:20)

Theme: Community and craftsmanship

Messianic Connection: The body of Messiah builds the spiritual house (1 Peter 2:5)

Haftarah: 1 Kings 7:40–50 | Brit Chadashah: Romans 12:4–8
Psalm 22

Mar 21 – Parashat #23 Pekudei (Exodus 38:21–40:38)

חזק חזק ונתחזק

Hazak, hazak, v’nit’chazek!
Be strong, be strong, and let us be strengthened!

Theme: Completion and glory

Messianic Connection: Messiah completes the dwelling of God among us (John 17:4; Revelation 21:22–27)

Haftarah: | Brit Chadashah: M’lakhim Alef (1 Kings) 7:51–8:21
Psalm 23
