אָבִינוּ שֶׁבַּשָּׁמַיִם


אָבִינוּ שֶׁבַּשָּׁמַיִם יִתְקַדַּשׁ שְׁמֶךָ׃
A-vi-nu she-bash-ah-ma-yim, yit-ka-dash she-meh-kha.

תָּבֹא מַלְכוּתֶךָ יֵעָשֶׂה רְצוֹנְךָ בָּאָרֶץ כַּאֲשֶׁר נַעֲשָׂה בַשָּׁמָיִם׃
Tah-vo mal-khu-teh-kha ye’ah-seh r’ts-o-neh-khha ba’ah-rets ka’a-sher na’ah-sah vash-ah-mah-yim.

תֶּן־לָנוּ הַיּוֹם לֶחֶם חֻקֵּנוּ׃
Ten-lay-nu hai-yom leh-chem chu-kei-nu.

וּסְלַח־לָנוּ אֶת־אַשְׁמָתֵנוּ כַּאֲשֶׁר סֹלְחִים אֲנַחְנוּ לַאֲשֶׁר אָשְׁמוּ לָנוּ׃
u’se-lach-lay-nu et-ash-may-tei-nu ka’ah-sher so-lech-im a-na-choo-nu la’ah-sher ash-mu lah-nu.

וְאַל־תְּבִיאֵנוּ לִידֵי מַסָּה כִּי אִם־הַצִּילֵנוּ מִן־הָרָע כִּי לְךָ
ve’al-teh-vey-e-nu ley-dei mas-sah ki im-hat-see-ley-nu min-ha-rah Ki la-cha

הַמַּמְלָכָה וְהַגְּבוּרָה וְהַתִּפְאֶרֶת לְעוֹלְמֵי עוֹלָמִים אָמֵן
hah-mam-lah-cha veh-ha-gee-vu-rah vee-ha-ti-fey-ret l’o-lee-mee o-lah-mim. Amen






Our Father in heaven! May Your name be kept holy.

May Your Kingdom come, Your will be done on earth as in heaven.

Give us the food we need today.

Forgive us what we have done wrong, 
as we too have forgiven those have wronged us.

And do not lead us into hard testing, but keep us safe from the evil one.

For kingship, power, and glory are Yours forever. Amen
