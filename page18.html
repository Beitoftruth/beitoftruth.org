Our Beliefs

Beit Emet

Beit Emet means the House of Truth. We are a Karaite Messianic Congregation who believe in Yeshua HaMashiach. He is our Redeemer. We are His followers. We study the teachings of Moses, the Prophets, the Psalms and the Apostolic Writings from a cultural and historical stance while applying those same teaching to everyday life.

Statement of Faith

There is one God and his name shall be one. He has many titles in Judaism. Each title that God has given us in the Hebrew Bible describes an aspect of his nature without voiding any part of who he is.
